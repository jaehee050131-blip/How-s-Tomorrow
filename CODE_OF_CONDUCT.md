# Code of Conduct

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) 2.1 (CC BY 4.0), shortened for a small student project.

## Our pledge

Everyone should be able to take part without harassment, whatever their background or experience. We are students, so we expect mistakes and questions.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and kind, especially in code review.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Accept feedback openly, and explain your reasoning when you disagree.
- Ask for help, and help others.

## What we do not accept

Insults, personal attacks, discriminatory language, harassment, sharing someone's private information, sexualized language, or anything else inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

The repository, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions, and the team chat we use for this project.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behavior that breaks this Code of Conduct, report it privately. Please do not reply in a public thread or the group chat.

1. **Send a report** in one of these ways:
   - Email `roy115@naver.com` (read by `JunbeomJang115`).
   - Send a direct message (not the group chat) to a maintainer on GitHub or in the team chat.
2. **Include** what happened, where (a link or screenshot helps), when, and who was involved.
3. **Acknowledgement:** a maintainer replies within 3 days to confirm they received it.
4. **Review:** maintainers who are not involved in the report discuss it and decide what to do. We keep reports private and share details only with the people who need them to handle it.
5. **Outcome:** maintainers may warn a person, remove comments or contributions, or limit repository access. We tell the reporter what was decided.

Reports about a maintainer go to another maintainer. A maintainer who is involved in a report does not take part in deciding it.
