# OSS-Project
2026 2학기 OSS 프로젝트
Sometimes you want to tell someone special, “It’s going to rain tomorrow, don’t forget your umbrella.” But reaching out first can feel awkward, and sending a weather app screenshot or a short text can seem low-effort and rarely starts a conversation. 내일 어때용? (How’s tomorow?) lets you pick a city and create a link where four cute dragon brothers deliver tomorrow’s weather, so you can show you care without the pressure.
